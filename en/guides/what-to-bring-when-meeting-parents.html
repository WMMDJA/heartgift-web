Relationship gifts

What to bring when meeting parents

The first meeting is about respect, not showing off. Bring something polite, easy to accept, and not so expensive that it creates pressure.

Safe choices

  • Fruit, tea, pastries, or a small food gift that can be shared.
  • A local specialty from your hometown or current city.
  • A modest health or comfort item for elders if you know it is appropriate.
  • Flowers only if the family likes them and there is no custom concern.
  • A small gift for younger siblings or children if they will be present.

What to avoid

Avoid overly expensive items, strong personal-care products, gifts that reveal too much personal preference, and anything your partner says the family dislikes. Ask your partner for food restrictions first.
