Holiday gifts

Lunar New Year visiting gifts

When visiting relatives during Lunar New Year, the goal is simple: do not arrive empty-handed, and do not make the gift feel awkward. Choose something shareable, useful, or easy for the household to enjoy.

Reliable gift categories

  • Fruit, snacks, nuts, or baked goods for the whole family.
  • Tea, coffee, wine, or a regional specialty when you know the household's taste.
  • Small gifts for children: books, stationery, puzzles, or age-appropriate toys.
  • Useful gifts for elders: warm socks, wellness items, or simple daily upgrades.
  • A local specialty from your city if you want the gift to feel personal.

What to avoid

Avoid perishable items that are hard to store, gifts that require complicated setup, and anything that might conflict with local customs. When unsure, choose a shareable food gift plus a warm greeting.

Reminder tip:

Create a HeartGift reminder for each family visit and note who has children, elders, or dietary preferences.
