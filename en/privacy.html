Privacy

Your relationship data stays in this browser

The first version of HeartGift Web does not require an account, does not upload contacts, and does not sync relationship notes to a server.

What is stored

When you add a relationship, the data is saved in your current browser's localStorage. Clearing browser data, switching browsers, or changing devices may remove it.

What HeartGift does not do

  • It does not read your contacts.
  • It does not require login.
  • It does not upload your relationship notes.
  • It does not send your form entries to third-party services.

Future ads and affiliate links

If ads or affiliate links are added later, this page will be updated to explain the relevant third-party cookies or tracking technologies.

Use it calmly

You can try sample data first, and you can remove local records by clearing browser data.
