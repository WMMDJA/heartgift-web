About

Relationships deserve to be remembered before the last minute

HeartGift Web is a lightweight relationship reminder and gift helper. It helps you save birthdays, anniversaries, preferences, gift ideas, and greeting messages in one calm place.

Why start with the web version

A web version is easy to publish, easy to share, and strong for search traffic. It also gives us a better path toward gift guides, affiliate links, and later mobile apps.

Who it helps

  • People who forget birthdays and anniversaries.
  • People who want thoughtful gift ideas without overthinking.
  • People who want to remember what family, partners, and friends actually like.

Content plan

HeartGift will keep adding practical birthday, anniversary, and holiday gift guides.
